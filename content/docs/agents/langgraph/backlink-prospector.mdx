## Summary

The **Backlink Prospector** identifies high-quality backlink opportunities by comparing competitor backlink profiles against your project. It finds sites linking to 2+ competitors but not your project, resource page link opportunities, guest post targets, and broken link reclamation opportunities. Results are ranked by domain authority and relevance.
