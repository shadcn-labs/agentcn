## Summary

The **Stripe Pulse** agent monitors your Stripe account for revenue health and identifies anomalies. It tracks unusual patterns like sudden drops in revenue, increased failed payments, subscription churn spikes, or unusual transaction volumes, then provides clear alerts and actionable insights.
