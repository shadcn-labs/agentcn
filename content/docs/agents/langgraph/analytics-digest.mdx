## Summary

The **Analytics Digest** creates concise weekly digests from PostHog data. It focuses on top events by volume and growth rate, unusual spikes or drops in key metrics, user cohort trends (new vs returning), feature adoption rates, and funnel conversion changes. Presents findings as a structured digest with key takeaways and action items.
