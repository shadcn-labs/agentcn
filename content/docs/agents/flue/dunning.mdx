## Summary

The **Dunning Agent** reviews failed Stripe payments and drafts recovery actions.
It categorizes failures by reason (expired card, insufficient funds, etc.) and
recommends next steps: automatic retry, customer outreach email, or account
suspension. Drafts personalized recovery emails when appropriate.
