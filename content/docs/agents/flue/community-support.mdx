## Summary

The **Community Support Agent** reads Discord messages, triages them by urgency
and topic, drafts helpful responses for unanswered questions, and flags issues
that need maintainer attention. Reach for it to keep your community responsive
without manual monitoring.

## Customization

- **Add auto-reply.** Wire in a Discord reply tool for instant responses.
- **Scope by channel.** Filter messages by channel ID or category.
- **Swap the model.** Edit `agents/community-support.ts`.
