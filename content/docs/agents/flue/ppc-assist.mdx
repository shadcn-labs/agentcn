## Summary

The **PPC Assist** analyzes campaign data from PostHog and Google Ads APIs to
identify optimization opportunities. It evaluates metrics like CTR, CPC,
conversion rate, and ROAS, then suggests bid adjustments, budget reallocations,
and ad copy improvements to maximize campaign performance.
