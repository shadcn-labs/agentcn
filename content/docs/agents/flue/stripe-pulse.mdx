## Summary

The **Stripe Pulse Agent** uses the Stripe CLI to pull payment data, subscription
metrics, and customer churn. It identifies revenue trends, anomalies, and
actionable insights, presenting findings with clear summaries and flagging
anything that needs immediate attention.
