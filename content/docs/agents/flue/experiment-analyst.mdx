## Summary

The **Experiment Analyst** reads PostHog A/B experiment results and summarizes
learnings. It calculates statistical significance, identifies winners, and for
each experiment reports variant performance, sample size, confidence level, and
business impact. Experiments needing more time or sample size are flagged.
